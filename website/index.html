How it works

Zero setup. Just hit play.

No plugins to configure and no accounts to link. yourLyrics listens directly to your operating system's now-playing feed.

1

Play from anywhere

Start a song in Spotify, Apple Music, or your browser. yourLyrics reads playback state via MediaRemote (macOS), SMTC (Windows), or MPRIS (Linux).

2

Instant word-level sync

Timed TTML, QRC, ELRC, and LRC lyrics load automatically with spring-driven word highlights, backing vocals, and instrumental interludes.

3

Customize your stage

Switch between macOS Liquid Glass, iOS, iPadOS, and tvOS layouts, enable animated covers, or pin the window always-on-top.

UI Styles

Four layouts, one player.

Switch between macOS, iOS, iPadOS, and tvOS styles anytime in Settings. Window chrome, Liquid Glass controls, artwork framing, and typography adapt to match.

Features

Built for listening.

Synced karaoke lyrics, live motion covers, and audio-reactive shaders tuned for smooth 60 fps playback.

Word-synced karaoke & backing vocals

Fetches TTML, QRC, ELRC, and LRC lyrics from LyricsFlow, Better Lyrics, LRCLib, and community sources with duet and credit support.

Animated album artwork

Streams animated motion covers when available, showing static artwork immediately with variable blur edges while streams load.

Audio-reactive mesh backdrop

WebGL2 mesh reacts to bass, mid, and high energy from your live audio output, plus an Old Apple Music backdrop toggle.

Liquid Glass & Discord Presence

Refractive Liquid Glass controls, Discord Rich Presence, tap-to-seek lyrics, and automatic power-saving pause when hidden.

FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need an Apple Music subscription?

No. yourLyrics reads whatever is currently playing on your computer — Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube Music in your browser, or local audio players.

Is yourLyrics free and open source?

Yes. It is completely free, contains no ads or tracking, and is open-sourced under the GNU General Public License v3.0 (GPL-3.0).

How well does it run on Windows and Linux?

macOS is the primary tested platform. Windows and Linux installers are built automatically for every release, though they receive less testing and may have minor platform quirks.

Does it drain battery or overheat my laptop?

The ambient backdrop is capped at 30 fps and pauses automatically whenever playback stops or the window is hidden or minimized.
