### Added
- **Share Lyrics**: Long-pressing a lyric line opens a Share Lyrics overlay with multi-line selection, spring-animated transitions, and Copy Image / Save PNG export.
- **Direct Unison (Better Lyrics) integration**: Queries `unison.betterlyrics.org` directly right after LyricsFlow Community, handles `- Topic` artist names, and resolves submitter attribution.
- **Clickable `Synced by` attribution**: Pressing the `Synced by` line in the lyrics view or the `Synced by` button in the lyrics sheet opens the curator's profile URL for Unison (`unison.betterlyrics.org/curator/...`) and LyricsFlow Community (`trylyricsflow.com/...`).
- **Provider Discord links**: Added `Join LyricsFlow on Discord` and `Join Better Lyrics on Discord` buttons to the lyrics sheet, and credited all lyrics providers in `README.md`.

### Improved
- Renamed the Unison/Better Lyrics provider label to **Unison (Better Lyrics)** while keeping the Discord button text focused on the server name.

### Fixed
- Fixed RTL duet alignment so answering Arabic/RTL duet lines align cleanly to the opposite edge.
